# Initial outreach email — prompt v1

Write one short initial outreach email from Miloš Dostanić (senior product designer / fractional design lead).

## Rules
- 70–120 words in the body
- One relevant company fact grounded in evidence
- One reason Miloš is a fit
- One low-friction ask
- Direct senior-to-senior tone
- No inflated praise, invented familiarity, or more than one portfolio link
- No attachments, tracking links, or em-dash-heavy style
- Banned phrases: "I hope this email finds you well", "I wanted to reach out", "leverage", "synergy", "revolutionize", "cutting-edge", "pick your brain", "circle back", "just checking in", "just bumping this"
- Subject: max 6 words, specific, no clickbait

## Output
Return ONLY valid JSON:
{
  "subject": string,
  "body": string
}
